# Fedory Vesper artwork

These four original, AI-assisted illustrations were generated for Fedory
with OpenAI image generation on 2026-08-01. They do not contain artwork from
upstream Omarchy themes.

The shared direction is a quiet cosmic-botanical world rendered in deep
aubergine, lavender, cyan, and silver. Each scene contains one restrained
optical spectrum detail tied to a physical surface:

- `01-glasshouse.webp` - refraction on glass
- `02-orbital-lilies.webp` - a caustic reflected in water
- `03-astral-canopy.webp` - diffraction at a translucent leaf tip
- `04-seed-observatory.webp` - a glint on a crystal lens

The selected masters were cropped to 16:9, resized to 2560x1440, stripped of
metadata, and encoded as quality-92 WebP files for distribution.
